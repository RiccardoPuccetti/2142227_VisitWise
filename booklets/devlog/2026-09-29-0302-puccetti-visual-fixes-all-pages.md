# PUC-13 - Visual fixes on every page

- **Author:** Puccetti
- **Task / user stories:** PUC-13; US-38, with US-09, US-13..US-18, US-26..US-30 for the pages touched
- **What changed:** A pass on every page after the restyling:
  - **Imports:** the cards show imported, located and skipped rows with a located bar.
  - **Map dashboard:**
    - charts above the map;
    - filters in a rail beside the map;
    - the selected point in a popup on its marker, and the map recentres on it;
    - clicking a marker opens its row in the list;
    - the list is paged with a shared pager (`TablePager`, `createPaging`);
    - wheel zoom without clicking the map first;
    - no chart flicker while filters load.
  - **Import detail:** the same layout as the map, with the paged points table.
  - **Planner:**
    - Setup and Plan tabs, with a short planning animation;
    - the plan days as a timeline of agent routes;
    - save the scenario from a dialog;
    - the geocoding status as a slim strip, without the plan note that repeated it;
    - equal setup cards, and step 1 no longer grows when step 2 does;
    - average speed and road factor grouped as the fallback used when the road network (OSRM) is unavailable.
  - **What-if and scenarios:**
    - the form as one toolbar;
    - a fixed-size curve beside its table;
    - the saved scenarios as a rail beside the comparison.
  - **Saved plan:**
    - marked as a saved scenario, with a way back, and Scenarios stays current in the sidebar;
    - one week at a time with a pager and a strip of days, and only the selected day's stops;
    - readable stop cards (the ERP capitals shown as ordinary words by `readableName`);
    - the agent filter in the pager row.
  - **Key figures:** on every page they are a headline figure (with a bar when it is a share) and a short list of details (`KpiSummary`). The old `KpiCard` is removed.
  - **Page header:**
    - a title row with the actions, then a strip of facts with icons (`page-facts`);
    - no breadcrumb;
    - more page padding, and 28px between sections.
  - **Navigation below 1024 px:** an off-canvas drawer opened from a slim top bar.
- **Why / decisions:** Every change came from the team's review of the pages in the browser, often choosing between mockups. The choices:
  - **Scenarios:** the rail and main layout.
  - **Saved plan calendar:** a week pager (A).
  - **Key figures:** headline and details (C).
  - **Header:** the title row and facts strip (B, with icons and without a back arrow).
  - **Planner geocoding notice:** a slim strip (A).
  - **Planner day list:** a combination of two mockups.

  The breadcrumb was dropped because the sidebar (or the drawer) already shows where the page is. Buttons that repeated the sidebar were removed from the headers. The drawer reuses the sidebar markup: it is inert while closed, and makes the page inert while open. No new dependency.
- **Verification:** Tests written first for every change of behaviour:
  - paging and the table pager;
  - the map popup, `centerOn` and wheel zoom;
  - the list scroll on a marker click;
  - the planner tabs, the loading view and the day timeline;
  - the geocoding strip and the notes filter;
  - `readableName`;
  - the week pager and the day strip;
  - `KpiSummary` and the figures of each page;
  - the Scenarios section kept current on a saved plan;
  - the page header;
  - the drawer (open and focus, Escape, navigation).

  Each failed first because the behaviour or the code was missing. Full frontend suite: 311 tests, 0 failures. `ng build` succeeds with the initial bundle warning (593.79 kB) and the `app.css` warning (6.53 kB). Checked in the browser on the synthetic import at 375, 768, 1162, 1280, 1440 and 1920 px, in both themes where colours changed: no horizontal page scroll.
- **Slide note:** Every page now reads the same way: a title with its facts, the key figure first, then the content, with the same spacing everywhere. On a phone, the navigation slides in from the side.
